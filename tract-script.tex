\textbf{The loop.} Page 12 ends with a corner tag, \textsf{RETURN TO PAGE 3}. On a first pass, page 3 is an unexplained underwater interlude between the coalition and the argument. On the second pass it's the proof that the repairs on page 12 happened. The whales appear twice: from above on page 12, from below on page 3.

\section{Production conventions}

\textbf{Reporter reference.} Curly dark-brown hair with substantial volume, large black square glasses, rust-red blazer, cream blouse, spiral notepad, silver handheld microphone. Mature, skeptical, expressive face; not glamorous, not resembling any recognizable person. The cover and pages 5 to 11 are on model. Pages 2 and 12 drift toward short, straighter hair and should be regenerated against the sheet.

\textbf{Lettering.} All caps. Yellow rectangular caption boxes for narration, white balloons for speech, a red-on-yellow title banner across the top of each story page. Page 3's banner is tinted blue to match its palette.

\textbf{Recurring figures.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The hard-hat worker appears on pages 4, 5, 7 and 11, a quiet second face alongside the reporter.
\item The three billionaires are generic composites, never likenesses of real people. The fur-collar billionaire from the cover owns the bunker on page 11.
\item The man in the striped-band fedora on page 12 nods to Tom Landry, the tract's main formal source.
\item Page 9's preacher echoes the Sacred Ending preacher on page 2, holding a tablet instead of a Bible.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Crowds.} Diverse and unstereotyped throughout, especially at the checkpoint on page 8.

\textbf{Script notation.} Each panel's art direction is followed by its lettering: \textsc{Caption} for narration boxes, a character name for speech.
